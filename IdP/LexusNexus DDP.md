Dynamic Decision Platform

Dynamic Decision Platform Overview
The platform utilizes over 10000 data sources via a single API to assess risk through behavioral and device signals. It simplifies data acquisition by using a rules engine for automated decisioning.
Behavioral Risk Assessment Demonstrations
Live testing highlighted the ability to flag suspicious behavior, such as copy-and-paste actions or geographic anomalies. The system effectively distinguishes between authorized users and those using stolen credentials.
Evolving Digital Identity Strategy
The strategy shifts focus toward holistic digital identity verification rather than relying solely on device identification. This approach ensures effectiveness against evolving threats like sophisticated proxies and privacy-focused technology deprecations.
